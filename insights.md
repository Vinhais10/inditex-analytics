# Business Insights - Inditex 2023-2025

Analysis based on real Inditex financial data stored in PostgreSQL.

---

## 1. Zara's Dominance and Concentration Risk

Zara represents **70.4%** of group sales (EUR 28,051M of EUR 39,864M).

**Risk:** if Zara stalls, the entire group stalls. This is the classic "single point of failure" problem.

---

## 2. The Growth Paradox

| Brand | Sales (EUR M) | Growth YoY |
|---|---|---|
| Zara | 28,051 | +1.0% |
| Bershka | 3,286 | +12.2% |
| Stradivarius | 3,002 | +12.7% |
| Massimo Dutti | 2,019 | +3.0% |
| Pull&Bear | 2,546 | +3.1% |
| Oysho | 960 | +15.5% |

**The flagship is the slowest growing brand.** Smaller brands are growing 15x faster.

**Interpretation:** Inditex is successfully diversifying away from its flagship. This reduces long-term concentration risk.

---

## 3. Revenue Growth Is Slowing

| Year | Revenue (EUR M) | YoY Growth |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 35,947 | -- |
| 2024 | 38,632 | +7.47% |
| 2025 | 39,864 | +3.19% |

**The growth rate halved in 2025.** This is not a crisis but a signal: the company is entering a more mature phase.

---

## 4. Fewer Stores, More Revenue

| Year | Stores | Revenue (EUR M) |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 5,692 | 35,947 |
| 2024 | 5,563 | 38,632 |
| 2025 | 5,460 | 39,864 |

**Loss of 232 stores while revenue grew by EUR 3.9B.**

**Interpretation:** Inditex is closing underperforming physical stores and shifting to online + high-traffic flagship locations. This is the classic "fewer, better" strategy.

**Per-store revenue:**
- 2023: EUR 6.32M
- 2024: EUR 6.95M
- 2025: EUR 7.30M

**+15.5% productivity per store in 2 years.**

---

## 5. Geographic Concentration

| Region | Share |
|---|---|
| Europe (excl. Spain) | 51.3% |
| Americas | 17.8% |
| Spain | 15.9% |
| Asia and Rest of World | 15.0% |

**67.2% of sales come from Europe.** The company remains heavily dependent on the European market.

---

## 6. ABC Analysis of Brand Portfolio

| Class | Brands | Cumulative Sales |
|---|---|---|
| A | Zara, Bershka | 78.6% |
| B | Stradivarius, Pull&Bear | 14.0% |
| C | Massimo Dutti, Oysho | 7.4% |

**Class A (Zara + Bershka) generates 78.6% of sales.** Standard ABC rule: focus resources on Class A, monitor Class B, cut or test Class C.

---

## Summary

Inditex in 2025 is:

- **Stable but maturing** (revenue growth halved)
- **Diversifying** (small brands growing faster than Zara)
- **More efficient** (+15.5% per-store productivity)
- **Still Europe-heavy** (67% of sales from one region)

**Strategy to watch:** whether Oysho, Stradivarius, and Bershka can reach meaningful scale before Zara's growth stalls completely.
